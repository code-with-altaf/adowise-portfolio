## Why Adowise is Better Than Stan Store

### 1. Stan Store is a Storefront. Adowise is a Business.

Stan Store gives you a link-in-bio page with products on it. That's it. It's great for a quick setup, but it's not a business platform — it's a digital shelf.

Adowise gives you the **complete infrastructure to run a creator business**: marketing tools, audience management, multiple revenue streams, brand partnership opportunities, and analytics — all connected.

### 2. Stan Store Doesn't Work Well for Indian Creators

Stan Store is heavily optimized for US-based creators. Payment infrastructure, currency handling, and the platform's overall ecosystem are built around Western markets.

**Adowise is built with Indian creators in mind** — with Razorpay/UPI payment support, India-first pricing, and a creator ecosystem designed for the Indian market.

### 3. Adowise Has Brand Sponsorships. Stan Store Doesn't.

One of Adowise's most powerful features is the **Creator-Brand Marketplace** — where businesses can discover creators and run sponsorship campaigns. Stan Store has no equivalent feature.

For creators looking to grow beyond product sales and into brand partnerships, Adowise is the only platform that supports this natively.

### 4. Adowise Has Real Marketing Tools

Stan Store gives you a page. Adowise gives you a **growth engine**:

- **Instagram Comment-to-DM automation** — reply to comments automatically with your links
- **Sales funnels** — convert visitors into paying customers
- **Referral programs** — let your audience grow your business
- **Promotional campaigns** — run time-limited offers to drive urgency

Stan Store has none of these marketing features.

### 5. Adowise Supports Webinars Natively

Stan Store doesn't support webinars. Adowise does — with full registration management, live session hosting, and paid or free webinar options. For educators and coaches, this is a critical feature.

## When Would You Use Stan Store?

Stan Store makes sense if:
- You're a US-based TikTok/Instagram creator
- You want a **dead-simple link-in-bio store** with minimal setup
- You only sell 1-2 digital products and nothing else
- You have no plans to build a community, offer coaching, or partner with brands

But once your creator business grows beyond a simple storefront — **Stan Store will hold you back.**

## Stan Store vs Adowise: The Real Cost
