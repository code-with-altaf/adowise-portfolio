## Adowise Labs — The Company

**Adowise Labs** is a software, SaaS, and AI company founded by **[Mohammad Altaf](https://mohammadaltaf.dev)** in Paonta Sahib, Himachal Pradesh, India.

After years of working in software engineering — including at Tradylytics — I realized that both creators and local businesses face the exact same problem: technology is too fragmented and too expensive. Creators need 10 different tools. Businesses can't afford enterprise software. Both groups are underserved.

So I built **Adowise Labs** to solve both problems.

Adowise Labs provides:
- **Custom Web Applications** — built with Next.js, Node.js, and React
- **SEO & Digital Marketing** — for local and national businesses
- **E-Commerce & ERP Systems** — so businesses don't rely on expensive platforms like Shopify or Justdial
- **SaaS Products** — starting with Adowise.com
