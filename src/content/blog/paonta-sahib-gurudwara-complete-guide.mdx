## The History of Gurdwara Paonta Sahib

Gurdwara Paonta Sahib is one of the most revered Sikh shrines in the world, located on the picturesque banks of the Yamuna River in the Sirmaur district of Himachal Pradesh.

The Gurdwara was established in memory of **Guru Gobind Singh Ji**, the tenth Sikh Guru, who stayed here for over four years. It is here that he wrote a significant portion of the *Dasam Granth*. The name "Paonta" translates to "Space for a footrest," as it is believed the Guru's horse stopped here, and he decided to make it his home.

## Essential Information for Visitors

### 1. Address & Directions
**Gurdwara Paonta Sahib Address:** CJPC+2XR, Court Road, Main Bazar, Paonta Sahib, Himachal Pradesh 173025.
It is extremely easy to reach. From Dehradun, the **Paonta Sahib distance** is roughly 45 km, and it is a scenic 1.5-hour drive. From Chandigarh, it takes about 3.5 hours by road. 

### 2. Timings & Hours
**Gurdwara Paonta Sahib Hours:** The Gurdwara is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can visit at any time of the day or night to offer your prayers.

### 3. The Langar & Accommodation
The Gurdwara runs a massive 24/7 Langar (community kitchen) serving thousands of people daily for free. There are also clean and well-maintained rooms (Sarai) available for pilgrims to stay at a nominal cost.
