## 1. The Divine Langar at Gurudwara Paonta Sahib

You cannot talk about food in Paonta Sahib without mentioning the Langar at the Gurudwara. Served 24 hours a day with immense love and devotion, the simple meal of dal, roti, and kheer is easily the most soul-satisfying food you will ever eat. It is an absolute must-experience.

## 2. Kake Da Dhaba

If you are looking for authentic, spicy North Indian highway food, Kake Da Dhaba is a legendary spot. Famous for its Dal Makhani, Paneer Butter Masala, and hot tandoori rotis, it is the perfect stop for travelers heading towards Dehradun.

## 3. Premium Dining: The Solitaire Hotel

For a more refined dining experience, The Solitaire Hotel offers excellent multi-cuisine options. If you are looking for the **Paonta Sahib best price** for a luxury dining experience with family, their restaurant provides exceptional service and ambiance.

## 4. Local Sweets & Bakeries

Paonta Sahib has a rich tradition of local bakeries. **Deep Bakers Brown World** and **Amit Bakers** are highly rated for their fresh pastries, cakes, and quick snacks. Don't leave without trying the local Himachali sweets available in the Main Bazar.
