## What is Skool?

Skool is a **community and course platform** built around the idea that engaged communities drive course sales. It combines a discussion forum, a course library, and a gamification system (leaderboards, points, levels) into one platform. Skool was popularized by its association with Alex Hormozi and has attracted a large following of coaches, consultants, and online business owners — primarily in the US and Western markets.

[Adowise](https://adowise.com) is an **all-in-one creator monetization platform** built for Indian and global creators. Adowise is built specifically for the Indian creator market with full local payment support.

## Why Adowise is Better Than Skool for Most Creators

### 1. Skool is a USD-Only, US-Focused Platform

Skool charges **$99/month per community in USD** — that's approximately ₹8,300+ per month at current rates. For an Indian creator who is just starting to monetize their audience, this is a significant fixed cost before you've made a single rupee.

Beyond pricing, Skool's payment infrastructure, community culture, and platform ecosystem are heavily US-focused. Indian creators face real friction using Skool — from payment processing to finding an audience that's active on the platform.

**Adowise is built for India** — with INR pricing, UPI and Razorpay support, and a creator community designed for the Indian market.

### 2. Skool Does One Thing — Adowise Does Everything

Skool's pitch is powerful: "Build a community + deliver courses + gamify engagement." It does this well.

But Skool **stops there**. There's no 1:1 booking, no digital product sales, no webinar hosting, no brand deals, no Instagram automation, no sales funnels.

Adowise does everything Skool does for community and courses — **and** adds 5 more revenue streams on top.

### 3. Adowise Has a Brand Sponsorship Marketplace — Skool Has Nothing

Skool has zero infrastructure for connecting creators with brands. There's no discovery mechanism, no campaign management, no brand partnership tools.

Adowise's **Creator-Brand Marketplace** lets businesses actively find and hire creators for paid sponsorship campaigns. For creators with an audience, this can be their largest income stream — and only Adowise supports it natively.

### 4. Adowise Has Instagram Automation — Skool Doesn't

Skool gives you a community. Adowise gives you a **growth engine**.

Instagram Comment-to-DM automation means every comment on your Instagram post can automatically trigger a DM with your offer — converting passive engagement into paying community members and customers. Skool has nothing like this.

### 5. Adowise Supports Every Creator Business Model

Skool's model assumes: **community + courses = creator business**. This is true for some creators. But coaches need 1:1 booking. Educators need webinars. Experts need paid queries. Creators with audiences need brand deals.

Adowise supports all of it. Skool supports two things.
