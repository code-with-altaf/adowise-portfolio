Learnyst has been co-founded with Hitesh Choudhary (the popular coding educator) and is popular among technical educators and course-heavy creators. It focuses primarily on **building and delivering online courses**.

## Why Adowise is Better Than Learnyst for Most Creators

### 1. Learnyst is an LMS — Adowise is a Creator Business Platform

This is the fundamental difference. Learnyst is an **online school infrastructure tool** — you build your school on Learnyst, add your courses, and it handles delivery. That's valuable, but it's a narrow use case.

Adowise is a **creator business platform** — it handles every way you can monetize your knowledge. Not just courses, but 1:1 coaching, digital products, webinars, brand deals, memberships, and more.

If you only want to run an online course school — Learnyst is purpose-built for that. But if you want to build a **complete creator business** with multiple income streams, Adowise is built for that.

### 2. Learnyst Has No Brand Sponsorship Marketplace

Learnyst is focused entirely on the creator side — helping you build and sell courses. It has zero infrastructure for connecting creators with brands for paid sponsorships.

Adowise has a **Creator-Brand Marketplace** where businesses discover creators, run campaigns, and pay for sponsorships — all inside the platform. For creators whose income includes brand deals, Learnyst offers nothing.

### 3. Adowise Has Marketing Automation — Learnyst Doesn't

Learnyst helps you deliver courses efficiently. It doesn't help you **grow your audience or convert followers into customers**.

Adowise has:
- **Instagram Comment-to-DM automation** — turn comments into leads automatically
- **Sales funnels** — convert website visitors into paying students
- **Referral programs** — let students refer others and earn rewards
- **Promotional campaigns** — create urgency with time-limited offers

These tools are what separate creators who grow from creators who stagnate.

### 4. Adowise Supports 1:1 Mentorship — Learnyst Doesn't

Learnyst is built for **group learning** — courses, cohorts, and batches. It doesn't have a native 1:1 booking system for paid mentorship or consultation sessions.

Adowise has full 1:1 booking infrastructure — calendar management, paid session booking, video call integration, and coaching packages. For creators who earn through personal coaching, this is critical.

### 5. Adowise Has Digital Products — Learnyst Doesn't

Learnyst is course-first and only. You can't sell ebooks, templates, guides, presets, or other digital downloadable products on Learnyst.

Adowise supports a full digital product storefront alongside your courses — so you can monetize your expertise in every format.

## Where Learnyst Genuinely Excels

Learnyst's **DRM content protection** is genuinely industry-leading — it's one of the best platforms for preventing course piracy. For educators who have premium video content they need to protect, Learnyst does this better than most.

Learnyst's **student analytics and progress tracking** are also more advanced than typical creator platforms — making it excellent for educators who manage large student cohorts and need deep reporting.

The Hitesh Choudhary connection has also helped Learnyst build credibility specifically in the **coding and technical education** space.

---

## When Should You Use Learnyst?

Learnyst makes sense if:
- Your **only** business model is selling online courses
- You need **industry-leading content protection (DRM)** for premium video
- You primarily serve a technical/coding education audience
- You don't need brand deals, 1:1 sessions, or marketing automation

But the moment you want to grow beyond just a course school — Learnyst becomes too limiting.
