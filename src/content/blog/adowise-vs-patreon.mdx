## What is Patreon?

Patreon is a **membership and fan subscription platform** that lets creators earn recurring revenue from their most loyal fans. Fans (called "patrons") pay a monthly subscription to access exclusive content, behind-the-scenes material, early releases, or community access. Patreon is popular with YouTube creators, podcasters, musicians, and artists.

## What is Adowise?

[Adowise](https://adowise.com) is a **full creator monetization platform** that includes memberships — but also covers courses, digital products, 1:1 mentorship, coaching packages, webinars, and brand sponsorships. Where Patreon is built around the fan-subscription model, Adowise is built around the **expert and creator business model** — helping you monetize your knowledge across every possible revenue stream.

## Why Adowise is Better Than Patreon for Most Creators

### 1. Patreon Takes 8–12% of Your Revenue

Patreon charges between **8% and 12% of your earnings** depending on your plan (plus payment processor fees). On top of that, they charge patrons separately too.

If you earn ₹1,00,000/month from memberships on Patreon, you lose ₹8,000–₹12,000 every month to platform fees alone.

Adowise offers **creator-first pricing** designed to maximize what you keep.

### 2. Patreon is One-Dimensional. Adowise is Multi-Stream.

Patreon's entire model revolves around **monthly fan subscriptions**. If you want to also sell a course, offer 1:1 coaching, sell digital products, or run a webinar — you need other platforms.

Adowise supports all of these. You don't just have one income stream — you have seven.

### 3. Patreon Has No Knowledge Monetization Tools

Patreon is built for **content creators** — YouTubers, podcasters, artists. It's not built for **knowledge creators** — coaches, mentors, educators, consultants.

If you're an expert who teaches, coaches, or consults — Patreon has no tools for you. No courses. No 1:1 bookings. No coaching packages. No webinars.

Adowise was built specifically for **expert-driven creators** who monetize their knowledge.

### 4. No Brand Sponsorship Infrastructure on Patreon

Patreon has no way to connect you with brands. Brand deals are handled entirely outside the platform — you find brands yourself, negotiate yourself, manage campaigns yourself.

Adowise has a **Creator-Brand Marketplace** where businesses actively search for creators to sponsor. You get discovered, negotiate, and manage campaigns — all inside Adowise.

### 5. Adowise Has Marketing Tools. Patreon Relies on Your Existing Audience.

Patreon is a passive platform — your patrons come to you if they already know you. There's no built-in mechanism to grow your audience or convert new followers into patrons.

Adowise has **active growth tools**:
- Instagram Comment-to-DM automation
- Sales funnels to convert visitors
- Referral programs to grow through word-of-mouth
- Promotional campaigns to drive urgency

## Where Patreon Still Excels

Patreon has a **massive existing creator and patron community**. For established YouTubers and podcasters who already have a large, loyal fan base — Patreon's name recognition can still drive patrons to their page.

Patreon's community features and **fan relationship tools** are also well-developed for the traditional fan-creator dynamic.

---

## When Would You Stay on Patreon?

Patreon makes more sense if:
- You're a YouTuber, podcaster, or musician whose audience already knows Patreon
- Your entire business model is **fan subscriptions** and nothing else
- You don't need courses, webinars, coaching, or brand deals

But if you're building a **modern creator business** with multiple revenue streams — Patreon is too narrow.
