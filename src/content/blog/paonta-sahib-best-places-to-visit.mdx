## 1. Gurudwara Paonta Sahib Ji

You cannot search for **"Paonta Sahib famous for"** without immediately seeing the iconic Gurudwara Paonta Sahib. Built on the banks of the Yamuna River, this sacred shrine holds immense historical significance. It is the very place where Guru Gobind Singh Ji spent over four years of his life. 

- **Highlights:** The golden palanquin (palki), the museum of historical weapons, and the serene view of the Yamuna River.
- **Tip:** The langar (community kitchen) serves free, delicious meals 24/7. 

## 2. Assan Barrage Bird Sanctuary (Asan Lake)

If you are looking for **tourist places near Paonta Sahib within 50 KMs**, the Assan Barrage (often called Asan Lake) is a must-visit. Located just at the border of Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, it is a paradise for bird watchers.

- **Activities:** Boating, paddling, and spotting rare migratory birds during the winter months.
- **Best time to visit:** October to March.

## 3. Simbalbara National Park

For nature enthusiasts, the Simbalbara National Park offers a quiet, untouched forest experience. Nestled in the Shivalik hills, it is perfect for a day trek. You can spot Goral, Sambhar, and Chital roaming freely in their natural habitat.

## 4. Gurudwara Shri Bhangani Sahib

Located about 23 km from Paonta Sahib, this Gurudwara marks the site of the Battle of Bhangani, the first battle fought by Guru Gobind Singh Ji. It is a highly significant historical site and offers a peaceful retreat away from the main town.
