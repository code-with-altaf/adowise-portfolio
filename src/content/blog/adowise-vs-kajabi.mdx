## The Big Problem with Kajabi: Price

Let's talk about the elephant in the room.

**Kajabi starts at $149/month** — that's approximately **₹12,000+ per month** just for the base plan. Their more popular plans go up to $399/month (₹33,000+/month).

For a creator just starting out — or even for an established Indian creator — this is an enormous cost. You'd need to generate thousands of dollars in revenue just to break even on your platform fee.

**Adowise is built to be accessible** — designed so creators can start earning without being crushed by platform fees from day one.

## Where Kajabi Has the Edge

In fairness, Kajabi is a mature platform with **highly advanced course-building tools**. Its video hosting, quiz systems, student progress tracking, and email automation are among the best in the industry.

If you're a US-based course creator with $10,000+ monthly revenue and want the most advanced course infrastructure available — Kajabi is arguably the best pure-course platform.

But "best course platform" doesn't mean "best creator platform overall."

Kajabi's digital product support is secondary to its course infrastructure. Adowise treats digital products as a first-class feature — ebooks, templates, guides, presets, resources — with a dedicated storefront and discovery mechanism.
