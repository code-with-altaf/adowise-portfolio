## What is Linktree?

Linktree is a **link-in-bio tool** that lets you create a simple landing page with multiple links. When someone clicks the link in your Instagram or TikTok bio, they land on your Linktree page and can choose where to go. That's it. Linktree's core value is simple: put all your links in one place.

Linktree has added some monetization features over time — tip jars, basic product links, and commerce integrations — but it remains fundamentally a **link aggregation tool**.

## The Core Problem with Linktree: It Sends Your Audience Away

Here's the fundamental issue with Linktree: **it's a middleman**.

When someone clicks your bio link and lands on Linktree, you send them to another website. If they click on your YouTube link, they go to YouTube. If they click on your course, they go to Teachable. If they click on your booking, they go to Calendly.

Linktree doesn't capture emails. It doesn't process payments. It doesn't build your audience. **It just redirects people away from you.**

All without ever leaving your Adowise page.
