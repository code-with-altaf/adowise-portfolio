## Why You Need a Direct Booking Website

Relying solely on third-party apps for your hotel or travel agency is a risky business model. A strong, independent digital presence offers several advantages:

### 1. Zero Commissions
When a customer books directly through your website, you keep 100% of the revenue. A well-optimized website pays for itself in just a few months of saved OTA commissions.

### 2. Build Your Own Brand
On MakeMyTrip, you are just one option in a list of hundreds. On your own website, you control the narrative. You can showcase high-quality videos of your rooms, your proximity to the Yamuna River, and the exact experience you offer.

### 3. Customer Data & Loyalty
When guests book through OTAs, the OTA owns the customer data. When they book through your custom portal, you get their emails and phone numbers, allowing you to run WhatsApp or Email marketing campaigns for their next visit.
