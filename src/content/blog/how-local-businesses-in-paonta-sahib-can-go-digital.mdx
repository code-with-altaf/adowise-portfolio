## Step 1: Claim Your Google Business Profile (FREE — Do This Today)

This is the **single most important thing** any local business can do. It's free, takes 15 minutes, and puts you on Google Maps.

### What is Google Business Profile?
When someone searches "restaurant near me" or "clinic in Paonta Sahib," Google shows a map with business listings. Those listings come from Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business).

### How to Set It Up
1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Enter your business name, category, and address
4. Add your phone number, website (if you have one), and hours
5. Upload at least 5 photos of your business
6. Verify (usually via postcard or phone call)

### What to Optimize
- **Business name:** Exact legal name (don't stuff keywords)
- **Category:** Choose the most accurate primary category
- **Description:** Write 2-3 sentences about what you do, mention Paonta Sahib
- **Photos:** Upload real photos — storefront, interior, products, team
- **Reviews:** Ask happy customers to leave Google reviews (this is HUGE for ranking)
- **Posts:** Share updates, offers, and events regularly

> **Pro tip:** Businesses with 20+ Google reviews and regular photo updates rank significantly higher in local search.

## Step 2: Build a Website

A website is your 24/7 digital storefront. Even a simple 3-page website (Home, About, Contact) is better than nothing.

### What Your Website Should Have

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| **Home** | What you do, where you are, your key selling point, call to action |
| **About** | Your story, team, experience, why customers should trust you |
| **Services/Menu** | What you offer with clear pricing if possible |
| **Contact** | Phone, WhatsApp, email, Google Maps embed, contact form |
| **Testimonials** | Real customer reviews and feedback |

### For Different Business Types

**Restaurants:** Online menu (with prices!), photos of food, Google Maps, WhatsApp for orders, Zomato/Swiggy links

**Hotels:** Room photos, pricing, amenities, booking form or WhatsApp booking, nearby attractions

**Clinics:** Doctor profiles, services, appointment booking, timings, patient testimonials

**Schools:** Admission information, courses, faculty, fee structure, parent portal

**Shops/Retail:** Product catalog, store location, delivery information, WhatsApp for inquiries

Having a website is not enough. You need people to **find** it when they search on Google.

### What is Local SEO?
Local SEO is the process of optimizing your online presence so that your business appears in local search results. When someone in Paonta Sahib searches "best dentist near me," local SEO determines which dentists show up.

### Local SEO Checklist for Paonta Sahib Businesses

- ✅ "Paonta Sahib" mentioned naturally on your website

- ✅ Google reviews — aim for 20+ genuine reviews

## Step 4: Social Media — Instagram & Facebook

For local businesses, **Instagram and Facebook** are the most effective social platforms.

### Instagram Strategy for Paonta Sahib Businesses

1. **Create a business account** with your business name
2. **Use location tags** — always tag "Paonta Sahib" in every post
3. **Use local hashtags:** #PaontaSahib #PaontaSahibFood #PaontaSahibBusiness #Sirmaur #HimachalPradesh
4. **Post consistently** — at least 3 times per week
5. **Share Reels** — short videos get 3-5x more reach than photos
6. **Respond to DMs** — treat Instagram DMs like phone calls

### Content Ideas

- Behind-the-scenes of your business
- Customer testimonials (video!)
- Before/after (especially for salons, home decor, construction)
- Daily specials (restaurants)
- Tips related to your expertise
- Local Paonta Sahib content (festivals, events, landmarks)
