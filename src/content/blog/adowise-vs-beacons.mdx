## What is Beacons?

Beacons is a **link-in-bio platform** that has expanded to include digital product sales, courses, memberships, appointments, and email marketing. It's popular among creators who want a customizable profile page that also sells products. Beacons is known for its drag-and-drop page builder and AI-powered tools for generating media kits.

## What is Adowise?

[Adowise](https://adowise.com) is an **all-in-one creator monetization platform** built for creators and experts who want to build a serious online business. Adowise covers every revenue stream — digital products, courses, memberships, webinars, 1:1 mentorship, coaching packages — and uniquely offers a **Brand Sponsorship Marketplace** that connects creators with businesses for paid campaigns.

## Why Adowise is the Better Choice Over Beacons

### 1. Beacons is Still a Link-in-Bio at Heart

Despite adding many features, Beacons' core DNA is a link-in-bio tool. The platform is optimized for **social media profile pages**, not for running a serious creator business.

Adowise is built from the ground up as a **business platform** — not a bio page. Every feature is designed to help you grow revenue, engage your audience, and scale your creator business.

### 2. The Brand Sponsorship Marketplace — Beacons Has Nothing Like It

Adowise has a **built-in Creator-Brand Marketplace** where businesses can discover creators, browse their profiles, and hire them for sponsored campaigns.

Beacons' closest equivalent is an AI-generated media kit — a document you share manually. That's fundamentally different from a **live marketplace** where brands actively come looking for creators.

With Adowise, you don't chase brand deals — brands come to you.

### 3. Adowise Has Real Automation for Growth

Beacons doesn't offer Instagram Comment-to-DM automation. Adowise does — meaning when someone comments on your Instagram post, they automatically get a DM with your link, offer, or content.

Combined with sales funnels and referral programs, Adowise gives you a **marketing machine** that Beacons simply can't match.

### 4. Adowise Offers Webinars Natively

Beacons doesn't support webinars. For creators who teach through live sessions, workshops, or Q&A events, this is a major gap.

Adowise lets you host **paid or free webinars** with full registration management, attendee tracking, and integrated payments — all inside the platform.

### 5. Built for India, Not Just the West

Beacons is built for US/Western creators. Payments, currency, and the overall platform experience are designed around Western markets.

**Adowise is built specifically for Indian creators** — with local payment infrastructure, India-first pricing, and an understanding of how the Indian creator economy works.

## Where Beacons Has an Edge

Beacons' **AI-powered media kit generator** is a standout feature — creators can automatically generate a beautiful media kit showing their stats, audience demographics, and collaboration history.

Adowise doesn't have this yet (it's on the roadmap), but it more than compensates with superior monetization tools, brand marketplace access, and automation features.

## When Would You Use Beacons?

Beacons makes sense if:
- You want a **beautiful, customizable link-in-bio page** as your primary goal
- You need an **AI-generated media kit** to pitch brands manually
- You're a US-based creator comfortable with Western-focused platforms

But if you want a platform that **makes you money** rather than just looking good — Adowise is the stronger choice.
