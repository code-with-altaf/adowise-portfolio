## The Spiritual History: Gurudwara Paonta Sahib

Paonta Sahib is globally renowned as a sacred site for Sikhs. The town was founded by the tenth Sikh Guru, **Shri Guru Gobind Singh Ji**, in 1685. 

- **The Meaning of "Paonta":** The word "Paon" means feet, and "tika" means stable. It signifies the place where Guru Gobind Singh Ji's feet rested and became stable. He stayed here for over four years.
- **Literary Hub:** During his stay, the Guru composed major sacred texts, including parts of the *Dasam Granth*.
- **Key Attractions:** The central white-and-gold shrine features a pure gold palanquin (palki), a museum of historical weapons, and the famous langar (community kitchen). Don't miss the Shri Kavi Darbar Sahib and the Yamuna Temple located right behind the main complex.

## How to Reach Paonta Sahib

Paonta Sahib is exceptionally well-connected to major cities, making it an ideal location for tourism and business.

- **By Air:** The nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun, approximately 61 km away.
- **By Train:** Dehradun Railway Station is the closest major railhead, just 45 km away.
- **By Road:** The city is easily accessible via National Highway 7. It is 45 km from Dehradun and about 180 km from Chandigarh.

- **Taruwala:** Known for its educational institutions and growing residential complexes.
