Gumroad is a **digital product marketplace and storefront** that lets creators sell ebooks, music, software, templates, courses, and memberships. It's been around since 2011 and is known for its simplicity — create a product, share a link, get paid. Gumroad handles payments and file delivery.

It's built for creators who want to build a real, sustainable online business.

## Why Adowise Beats Gumroad for Most Creators

### 1. Gumroad is Only a Product Store — Adowise is a Business Platform

Gumroad's entire value proposition is: **sell files on the internet**. That's powerful, but in 2026, selling files is just one of many ways creators earn money.

Adowise supports **7+ revenue streams** — so you're not dependent on just digital product sales. 1:1 sessions, courses, webinars, memberships, brand deals — you can diversify and grow in ways Gumroad simply doesn't allow.

### 2. Gumroad Takes a Flat 10% Commission

Gumroad charges a **10% fee on every sale** — no monthly subscription, but that 10% adds up fast. On ₹1,00,000 in sales, you're giving Gumroad ₹10,000 just in platform fees (before payment processor fees).

Adowise is built to maximize your earnings with **creator-first pricing** — so you keep more of what you earn.

### 3. Gumroad Has No Services Layer

If you're a coach, consultant, or mentor — Gumroad can't help you. You can't offer paid 1:1 bookings, coaching packages, or webinars on Gumroad.

Adowise bridges the gap between **products and services** — letting you sell both on the same platform, to the same audience, from the same dashboard.

### 4. Zero Marketing Automation on Gumroad

Gumroad gives you a link. You drive all your traffic yourself. There's no Instagram automation, no funnels, no referral programs — none of the tools that help you grow.

Adowise has a **full marketing suite** built in:
- Instagram Comment-to-DM automation
- Sales funnels and landing pages
- Referral and rewards programs
- Promotional campaigns and discounts

### 5. Brand Sponsorships — A Revenue Stream Gumroad Ignores

Gumroad has no mechanism for brand partnerships. Adowise has a **Creator-Brand Marketplace** where businesses discover creators and run paid sponsorship campaigns.

For creators with an audience, brand deals can be the biggest revenue stream — and Adowise is the only creator platform that facilitates this end-to-end.

## When Would You Use Gumroad?

Gumroad still makes sense if:
- You want to sell a **single digital product** with no ongoing platform costs
- You need something **extremely simple** to set up in minutes
- You're selling to an international audience and want a familiar checkout experience

But Gumroad becomes a ceiling the moment you want to grow beyond basic product sales.
