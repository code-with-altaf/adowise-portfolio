With Adowise, **brands come to you.**

TagMango focuses on content delivery — workshops, courses, and communities. It doesn't have marketing automation to help you grow.

TagMango is a platform for delivering content. Adowise is a platform for **growing and monetizing** your business.

### 3. Adowise Supports More Revenue Streams

TagMango is heavily focused on workshops, courses, and communities. Adowise covers the complete creator revenue stack:

- Digital products (ebooks, templates, guides)
- Paid queries / AMA
- Coaching packages
- Brand sponsorships
- Referral programs

All of these are revenue streams TagMango doesn't fully support.

### 4. Adowise Has a Paid Queries Feature — TagMango Doesn't

TagMango's entire DNA is built around **edutainment creators** — people who teach things through social media. This makes it excellent for educators and course creators, but limited for other types of experts.
