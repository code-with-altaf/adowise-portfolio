## The Core Industries of Paonta Sahib

Paonta Sahib's economy is driven by several key sectors:

### 1. Pharmaceutical Manufacturing
Paonta Sahib is a major pharma hub, with dozens of companies manufacturing medicines for domestic and international markets. 
**The Digital Need:** Pharma companies need robust ERP systems to manage inventory, track batch expirations, handle compliance documentation, and manage B2B distributor portals.

### 2. Cement and Heavy Manufacturing
With its proximity to raw materials in the Shivalik range, cement manufacturing is a cornerstone of the local economy.
**The Digital Need:** Heavy industries require custom software for logistics tracking, fleet management, and employee attendance tracking across large-scale factory floors.

### 3. FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods)
Several prominent FMCG brands have set up their manufacturing units in the Kyarda Doon valley due to favorable government policies.
**The Digital Need:** These companies require e-commerce portals, supply chain management systems, and data analytics dashboards to forecast demand.
